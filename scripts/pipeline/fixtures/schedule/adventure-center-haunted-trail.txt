The Haunted Trail is created and brought to life by Asheville Plays. This event is ideal for Halloween lovers of all ages, however, kids age 3-12 is the suggested age range. This is the 6th year that the Adventure Center has hosted this event which is a one-of-a-kind live outdoor theatrical experience where guests are promised an entirely immersive and interactive spooky night out. Every year the show is loved by boils and ghouls from all over WNC and we hope to see you there! Read more The Haunted Trail The Haunted Trail is 18 live-action outdoor scenes with magical lights and spooky sounds portraying renditions of classic Halloween themes. It is a one-of-a-kind live theatrical experience on an outdoor trail without contact or gore. Geared towards families with kids ages 3-12, visit the rowdy cowboys, a mad doctor, silly clowns, and amazing characters along your way. For your little ghouls, The ‘Sweet Peek Tour’ is offered nightly at 6:30 pm, sponsored by Bo Jangles. This is a guided tour for younger guests and parents to see the Trail and understand how we make it family friendly. The trail is actor-free at this time and they can decide if they would like to re-enter the line to receive the full show. Aerial Glow Trail The Treetops Adventure Park Aerial Glow Trail is also available for guests 4 years or older! Climb on two of our aerial trails while they are illuminated with thousands of colorful twinkle and laser lights. Please review the Glow Trail Section below for age requirements and adult escorts. If you want to enjoy both of these amazing activities, combo passes are available and often sell out, so make sure you reserve your tickets early! *The Haunted Trail does not sell out, but the combo passes may and the entrance closes at 9 pm. The Glow Trail is not available on October 22. The Haunted Trail Festival Play while you wait! The line to enter the Haunted Trail surrounds the game field. Kids can pop out of line to participate in contests and play Asheville Play s Monster-Sized Games. PIRATES COVE – Visit our 30-foot Pirate Ship! If you are up for an escape game, visit the Pirate Ship Escape Room to “test ‘yer wit mateys!” 10 voyages are available nightly for purchase with groups of up to 6 per voyage. Check in at the pirate ship for your escape time. Escape room voyages will be purchased onsite (no advanced tickets). Meet a live Mermaid for a photo opportunity and play swashbuckling games. NEW THIS YEAR – Area 50Fun – Prepare for an out-of-this-world dance party! Area 50Fun is where aliens, humans, and glow collide for an interactive cosmic celebration. Get ready for glow games, ridiculous dances, alien antics, and nonstop fun under the stars. Beam yourself over to Area 50Fun… if you’re brave enough! EAT, DRINK AND BE SCARY! Grab dinner onsite! Local food is available. Fresh Hot Donuts, Cotton Candy & Popcorn by Asheville Plays Jeremiah’s Ice S’mores for purchase to roast at the Bonfire Spookeasy serves Local Beer, Wine, and Spirits for the big ghouls Hot Chocolate & Cider Devils Foot Beverages & Sodas UNDER THE BIG TENT – Under the white tent you will find the concessions, the Little Ghouls Play Area (recommended for ages 4 and under), and meet our sponsors. Accessibility While the Haunted Trail Festival area is accessible by wheelchair or stroller, the actual Haunted Trail is not. Many of our “walking” areas are thick gravel. So small-wheeled strollers usually do not work well. Check-In and Cancellations Check-In Upon arrival at The Adventure Center of Asheville please follow the fence and lights to the Entrance. A greeter will send you to the correct place to check-in for either the Haunted Trail or Combo Pass. ***** Online Combo Pass Haunted Trail/Aerial Glow Trail waivers should be completed when making reservations to significantly reduce wait time at check-in. Giving Back We are dedicated to making The Haunted Trail a true community event and are honored to be a part of your holiday celebrations. BOO To Hunger – We say Boo To Hunger by donating 4 meals per ticket to MANNA Foodbank. In the past 5 years we have donated 120,000 meals to MANNA. Sensory Friendly Night – Halloween Is For Everyone! Sensory Friendly Night is sponsored by Camp Lakey Gap. This night will operate normally, however, there will be a special check-in booth for Sensory Friendly Night offering special services for those who request them. Also a $1 for every beer sold will go to Camp Lakey Gap. (Over $7,000 donated previously.) We also support Give Kids The Music (over $7000 donated to date) and The Boy Scouts. Groups and Birthday Parties You may bring a group or a Birthday Party to the Haunted Trail. Just buy your tickets via the links above. However, there is no further discount and no outside food or drink are allowed to be brought in after 5pm. Also, if your group has combo passes, you must check in together to be able to climb the Glow Trail at the same time. Your Haunted Hosts The Haunted Trail is produced by Asheville Plays!. Asheville Plays! was founded by a local husband and wife team, Arica Haro and Hans Banzer who create lasting memories through the power of play. The Haunted Trail is Asheville Plays! signature event, and they are striving to make The Trail a treasured part of your family’s tradition. For more information, visit www.hauntedtrailwnc.com . For more information about Asheville Plays!, visit www.ashevilleplays.com . *Sensory Friendly Night – October 25 Join us for a “Sensory-Friendly” walk through the Haunted Trail. In cooperation with Camp Lakey Gap Autism Programs, we will be providing a family-friendly Haunted Trail experience with special accommodations for youth or adults with sensory issues. How does Sensory Friendly Night work? The Haunted Trail will be operating normally on this night (we do not use strobe lights on the Trail.) If you want a sensory-friendly experience, check in at the Sensory Friendly Night booth beside Pirates Cove. It will be indicated with a banner, table, and counselors in Sensory Friendly Night t-shirts. The counselors will allow you to bypass the waiting line, and enter the Trail through a special entrance. On the Trail, the counselor can walk ahead of your group to alert the actors depending on your needs (i.e. no jump scares, etc.) You may also enter the Trail at 6:30 pm. During this time, it is not yet dark and there are no actors. Actors enter the Trail at 7 pm. FAQs Is it kid/family friendly? Yes! We take special care to introduce kids to Halloween without traumatizing them. We stick to classic Halloween (witches, pirates, etc.) There is no blood and gore. All scenes have a barrier and nothing will touch you or chase you. It is created for kids age 3-12. What time should I get there? Doors open at 6:00 in the Festival Area. The Haunted Trail “Sweet Peak” is at 6:30 – meaning no actors, just scenes. Then the Haunted Trail is open with actors from 7-9 pm. If you are climbing the Aerial Glow Trail, too, please arrive between 6-7 pm to check in and have time to climb the Aerial Glow Trail and walk the Haunted Trail. All trails close at 9 pm. Please plan time to park, check-in, and sign waivers. We are excited but a little nervous . Totally understandable. We have a Sweet Peek Tour at 6:30 before it gets dark. At this time, there are no actors on the trails, only scenes. Then if you feel comfortable, you may come back through later when actors are present. What is there to do other than The Haunted Trail? There is a festival area with Pirates Cove, Area50Fun, a game field, Little Ghouls play area and live contests. There will also be local food, donuts, popcorn, Jeremiahs Ice, local beer, wine and spirits, Devils Foot sodas, hot chocolate and hot cider for sale. And of course, the aerial Glow Trail (if it is not sold out.) What is Boo to Hunger? It is our way of giving back to our community. For every Haunted Trail Ticket, $1 is donated to Manna Food Back which turns into 4 meals. May I bring our own food & beverages? Food and Beverages are not allowed to be brought in after 5pm. If we buy a Combo Pass Ticket, do I need to fill out a waiver if we just came to the Adventure Center or Kolo Bike Park? Yes, the Combo Pass waiver includes the Haunted Trail waiver and a new one must be completed each year. The Haunted Trail Support Our Sponsors Aerial Glow Trail Rules and Regulations Climbers must be at least 4 years old, certain ages require an adult escort. Climbers must be 265 lbs. or less Climbers must have closed toed shoes that are secure and protect the feet. Climbers must attend training on safety gear, course equipment and structures within the park. Climbers must demonstrate the ability to perform specific transfer tasks independently. Climbers must be able to read, understand, and accept all park and ranger instructions in English. Climbers that are 18 years and older may escort or supervise younger climbers. Climbers must only use trails designated as appropriate based on age restrictions. Climbers must limit access to the course to authorized times during operating hours. Climbers must remain clipped to the safety system at all times while climbing on the course. Climbers must accept all risks involved in participation and sign a release of liability waiver Climbers must use equipment and gear properly as instructed and required by manufacturer. The Purple, Green, and Blue Trails will be the only trails available for this event.
