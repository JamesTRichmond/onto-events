This free show occurs on the first Saturday of each month. Find your way around the night sky by downloading a detailed monthly sky map from our friends at Skymaps.com. Skymaps.com 2nd Saturday - SCIturday 10 am - 2 pm Join us for family friendly activities featuring various STEAM topics (science, technology, engineering, art and math). Upcoming Topics October 10 - Animal Adventures 2nd Sunday - Storytime Under the Stars 6 pm - 7 pm Join us for an enchanting evening of nostalgia and wonder where you can enjoy classic bedtime stories in our planetarium under a celestial canopy of twinkling stars. A live narrator reads selected picture books with pages projected onto the Planetarium dome for families to enjoy the illustrations and follow along. 3rd Saturday - Kids' Free Day all day On Kids’ Free Day children 12 and under receive a free* ticket to any scheduled IMAX documentary or planetarium show with a paying adult. *Receive one complimentary child’s ticket for each adult ticket purchase.
